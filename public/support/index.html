How to play

Drag parts from the three sockets onto the 8×8 board. Fill a row or column to clear it. Tap Rotate under a part to turn it. The game ends when no part fits anywhere, in any rotation. Tap ? in the game for the full rules.

Questions

How do circuits work?
Fill the squares next to the + and − terminals beside the board and join them with filled squares touching edge to edge. The path and every filled square touching it clear. While you drag, they light up violet with a lightning mark.
When does the daily circuit change?
At midnight, your local time. Everyone gets the same starting board and parts, and you get one try a day. Finish it on consecutive days to build a streak.
How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Tap ? at the top of the game. Both switches are at the bottom of the panel.
Where are my stats?
Tap your peak score at the top left. You'll see games played, average score, streaks, circuits completed and more.
Can I restart a game?
Tap the restart button at the top right, then tap it again to confirm. The daily circuit has no restart.
Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the app removes your scores, stats and streak.
