Overclock

Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Overclock collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your game in progress, your peak score, today's daily circuit, your last daily result and streak, and your sound, vibration and help settings. They are stored only on your device, in the browser's local storage on the web. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes them.

Game updates

The iOS and Android apps check Expo's update service, EAS Update, for new versions of the game. According to Expo, that request carries your device's operating system, the app's project ID and a random token used to check for updates, and no personal information or device identifiers.

The web version

The web version is served by Expo's hosting service. As with any website, their servers receive the requests needed to deliver the page. The game itself sends nothing back.

Sharing

The Share button on a daily result opens your device's share sheet, or copies the result to your clipboard. Your result goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

Overclock collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. Questions: qgjameschen@gmail.com.
